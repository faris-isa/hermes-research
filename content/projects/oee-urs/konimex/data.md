# Data Requirements (1.7)

| ID | Requirement | Notes |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| 1.7.1 | System upgradeable for additional data | — |

## Report Requirements

- Printed reports include: timestamp, user who printed, watermark
- All reports exportable to Excel
